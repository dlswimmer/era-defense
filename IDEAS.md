# Ideas

Kids' suggestions for Era Defense, with who suggested them. Use the 💡 button in the game to
collect ideas on the device, then tap **Copy all** and paste the best ones here.

Status: `idea` → `planned` → `in game`. When an idea ships, add `by: '<name>'` to its entry in
`src/sim/data/` so the game shows the credit.
